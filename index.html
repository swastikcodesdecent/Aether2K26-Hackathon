// About Aether 2K26

WHERE STUDENTS
BUILD THE FUTURE

A 6-hour AI hackathon on school campus — where young innovators from Classes 9–12 transform real-world problems into intelligent, working solutions.

Aether 2K26 is an AI-focused hackathon hosted by De Paul School Kolkata, designed exclusively for students of Classes 9 through 12. In just six hours, participants form teams and race to build technology-driven projects that solve genuine, real-life challenges using creativity, code, and critical thinking.

The event is proudly powered by Vyronix Labs and co-powered by VMC Tech India — bringing professional-grade mentorship, infrastructure, and an electrifying competitive atmosphere right to the school campus. This is not just a competition; it's a launchpad for the next generation of problem-solvers and builders.

From ideation to final demo, Aether 2K26 challenges every participant to think beyond textbooks — sharpening their logical thinking, teamwork, coding skills, and presentation abilities under real pressure. The best ideas don't just win prizes — they prove that young minds can change the world.

Event A 6-hour AI hackathon held on the De Paul School Kolkata campus, open to Classes 9–12.
Powered By Vyronix Labs (primary) & VMC Tech India (co-power) — driving innovation at every level.
Goal Empower students to build real, AI-driven solutions for real-world problems — in one day.

Innovation

Push the boundaries of what's possible. Dream up, design, and deploy ideas that have never been built before — all in six hours.

Teamwork

Collaborate, communicate, and combine each member's strengths. The best solutions at Aether are always built together.

Real-Life Problem Solving

Tackle genuine challenges from everyday life. Your project must solve something that actually matters — not just pass a test case.

Presentation & Demo

Build it, then pitch it. Every team presents their solution live — judges evaluate your idea, execution, and how well you explain it.

// Competition Categories

TRACKS

Two tracks. One mission — build something that matters. Choose your arena and bring your best ideas to life.

// Track 01

Classes 9 & 10

Students of Classes 9 and 10 will build a project that solves a real-life problem. The primary tool is BlueJ — but if you know another programming language, you are absolutely free to use it. What matters is the idea and the impact.

BlueJ Java Any Language
Freedom to use any other programming language you are comfortable with. Creativity is the only requirement.

// Track 02

Classes 11 & 12

Students of Classes 11 and 12 can build projects using BlueJ, Python, or any other programming language they are comfortable with. Expect a higher bar for complexity, innovation, and technical depth.

BlueJ Python Java Any Language
Use the language you know best. Judges will evaluate your solution, not your syntax.

// How you'll be judged

Judging Criteria

01
Idea

Originality and creativity of the concept. Is it something we haven't seen before?

02
Real-Life Usefulness

Does it solve an actual problem? Would people genuinely benefit from this?

03
Execution

How well is the project built? Does it actually work and function as intended?

04
Teamwork

Did the team collaborate effectively? Does each member contribute meaningfully?

05
Presentation

How clearly and confidently is the project explained and demonstrated to the judges?
