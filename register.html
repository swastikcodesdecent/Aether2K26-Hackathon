Why AETHER?

More than a competition — a launchpad for your ideas.

6-Hour Sprint
An intense, high-energy marathon that pushes your limits and forces creative breakthrough under real constraints.
Team of Two
Partner up with one collaborator. Tight teams mean clearer communication, sharper focus, and faster execution.
Real Recognition
Compete for trophies and certificates. Top performers earn prestigious recognition and bragging rights.
All Classes Welcome
Open to students from Class 9 to 12. Whether you're a veteran coder or curious beginner — there's a place for you.
Digital Identity
Every participant gets a unique ID and an encrypted QR entry pass — your digital passport to the event.
Fully Online
Compete from anywhere in India. All you need is a laptop, a browser, and the drive to build something legendary.
